## Design Context

### Users
GigWork.ht serves customers in Haiti who need reliable local services, workers who need a trustworthy channel for paid work, and operators who need to monitor trust, disputes, payments, and supply coverage.

### Brand Personality
Grounded, capable, and protective. The interface should feel local and practical, not like a generic global gig clone.

### Aesthetic Direction
A bright product UI with Haiti-inspired blue/red anchors, warm paper surfaces, strong editorial typography, and dense but readable marketplace controls. The first screen should be the working product, not a landing page.

### Design Principles
- Lead with useful marketplace action: search, categories, trust signals, and service cards.
- Make Haitian Creole first-class, with clean UTF-8 copy and easy language switching.
- Show role and permission boundaries visibly so auth/authorization are part of the product story.
- Treat security, stats, payments, and mobile launch as product surfaces, not hidden TODOs.
- Keep mobile layouts ergonomic for unstable networks and repeated field use.
